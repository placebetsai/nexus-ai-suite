## 3. PlaceBets — done and live

Commits (repo `Placebetsai-src`): `3f3da12`, `7ddb924`, `6480223`. Deployed to `placebetsai` (PlaceBets acct).

### New tools (the "DraftKings would buy this" track)
- **`/ev` — +EV finder.** `lib/edges.js`: fair odds = median no-vig price across every book on the same market/number; EV = fair × best price − 1. Longshots (+1000 and up) listed separately; one-book outliers >12% EV flagged as **stale lines, never picks**. Live: 672 markets, ~9–17 +EV bets, 4–7 flagged.
- **`/movers` — real line movement** (open → consensus). Previously it relabeled the model-vs-market gap as "where the line moved". Live: 60 moves.
- **`/books-vs-kalshi`** — sportsbook fair price vs Kalshi bid/ask, gap in points, Kalshi EV after fee (0.07·P·(1−P)); 5¢–95¢ only (tick noise). `lib/kalshi-games.js`, matcher unit-tested 16/16 (Dodgers "Los Angeles D", New Mexico vs New Mexico St., Miami (OH)/(FL)); 81–82 games matched **locally**.
- **Best-price share** panel on `/ev`: DraftKings had the best price on 23–31% of markets (top or tied-top) — useful pitch stat.
- `/api/edges` serves all three, 60s edge cache (9.9s → 7ms locally). Navbar: +EV, Line Moves, vs Kalshi.
