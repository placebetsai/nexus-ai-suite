How it works

Three moves from idea to link.

1

Describe it

Type what the app should do in plain words, or start from an example and edit the first sentence. The team plans it, then writes the files.

2

Check it

Your app appears in the preview pane next to the chat. Switch between phone and desktop, and keep describing changes until it looks right.

3

Put it online

Press Put online to get a web address anyone can open, keep the files in The code, or send them to your GitHub account.

    Grey means it hasn't happened yet. Blue means you've typed something and it's ready to send — that's only the sentence in step B. Green means the site really answered a moment ago, and every number printed under the step was read straight out of that answer: how long it took, how many bytes, how many files, and whether the address worked.
