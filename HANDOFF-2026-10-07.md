## What's live
- Homepage/sports/daily-picks/track-record/predict: 2026 heroes (rotating gradient headline, free badge, league logo bg). One merged ticker (live scores → odds → news headlines), refresh 30s, pure CSS marquee (cannot runaway).
- Chat bot `/api/chatbot` (`lib/llm/model-pick.js`): game/team asks → pick + model % + no-vig % + edge + verdict + reasons + ESPN injuries + news (ESPN/Google News, source+time ET). "best pick today" → top 3 by edge. Tutor answers (odds, spread, EV, bankroll, vig, Kelly...). Record answers from graded ledger.
- `/my-bets`: bet tracker in localStorage (no login on site); auto-grades board moneylines via `POST /api/scores` (ESPN final scores); ROI by sport, leaks (groups ≥3 bets with ROI < -10%).
- `/pickem`: weekly NFL pick'em vs AI (slate + AI picks frozen per week in edge cache), D1 `pickem_entries`, leaderboard, canvas share card (Web Share / download).
- Affiliate slots (`components/v26/AffiliateSlots.js`, `lib/affiliates.js`): DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, ESPN BET, BetRivers → `/api/go?book=..&src=..` logs D1 `affiliate_clicks`, 302 to the book. **PLACEHOLDERS: affiliateUrl = null for all books** (plain homepages). Activate by setting `affiliateUrl` or env `AFF_<KEY>_URL`.
- PWA: manifest (id/scope/shortcuts), icon-192/512, apple-touch-icon, sw.js cache v3 with push + notificationclick handlers.
- Everything free. No paid tiers/pricing.
- D1 migrations applied to prod: `0011_pickem_affiliate.sql`, `0012_push.sql` (push_subscriptions, push_runs).
- Ticker news filtered to `category === "sports"` (`components/GlobalTicker.js`); Bloomberg/MarketWatch/CNBC/Wikipedia items dropped.
- Bot off-board picks (`buildOffBoardPick` in `lib/llm/model-pick.js`): team not on the odds board → next game from ESPN team schedule → "Pick: X to win. Model win probability N%.
